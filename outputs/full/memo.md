## Limitations

Reviews are self-selected and historical, so they may not represent all users or the current app. Classification is automated; 100316 reviews [F.needs_review] were flagged as needing review, and 13 [F.quarantined] were quarantined. Priority score here equals severity sum, so it favors high-volume issues. These data do not show causal effects, revenue impact or churn. Quotes are illustrative examples only.
